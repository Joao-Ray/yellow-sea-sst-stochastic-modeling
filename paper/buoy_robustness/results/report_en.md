# Retrospective buoy support sensitivity

A later descriptive platform-support sensitivity retained thirty-day ExtraTrees gains of 11.20–13.29% after removing each of 25 nighttime platform type/ID combinations, compared with 12.11% originally. Removing an entire platform type gave 11.76–18.95%; equal total weight per platform over the period gave 14.10%. These are different observed-support summaries, not uncertainty intervals or new independent validation (Supplementary Section S15).

The same support sensitivity gave thirty-day anomaly ridge/tree gains against grid source AR(1) of -24.21 to -17.78% and -25.77 to -18.43% across single-platform removals, respectively; whole-type removals gave -24.02 to -11.10% and -24.53 to -13.12%. All removals, vanished-date counts, same-retained-date references and alternative period-platform weights are reported in Supplementary Section S15.
